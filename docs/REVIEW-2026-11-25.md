## Critical issues and risks to decide on

1. **No real backups on the free Supabase plan.** The script is a stopgap. Decide: upgrade to Supabase Pro (daily backups) before real learners and payments, and approve a scheduled weekly run of the backup script on your computer until then. Both need your OK.
2. **Project pausing.** Free projects pause after about a week of no activity. The keep-alive helps, but depends on GitHub's scheduler (switches off after 60 days with no commits). A paused project makes sign-in fail for everyone.
3. **Email sending limits.** The default Supabase sender is heavily rate-limited, so sign-up and password-reset emails will fail when many testers join. Needs your own SMTP sender (a domain email). Decision and a small cost.
4. **Data retention (ADR-006) is still a draft.** Sign-in and activity records are deleted after 13 months by `fd_activity_purge`, but that function is **not scheduled** yet, and the active-time records have no purge rule. Confirm the retention periods (certificate evidence longer) and I will schedule it. The privacy page already promises 13 months for sign-in and activity records.
5. **Privacy page and terms are drafts.** I updated the privacy page for activity tracking. It still says answers are not saved to accounts, which may no longer be true now that progress and attempts are saved. Needs your wording review (and ideally a lawyer) before public launch under the Kenya Data Protection Act.
6. **Admin recovery is SQL-only.** If the master admin loses their authenticator app and password access, the only way back is directly in the database. Write down the recovery steps and keep a second admin with 2-step set up.
7. **If Supabase two-step (TOTP) is ever switched off, admins lose access to /admin**, since 2-step is mandatory for admins. Do not disable it in the dashboard.
8. **The catalogue page is static and still lists locked missions.** Learners can see titles they cannot open. Decide whether that is wanted (a shop window) or whether locked ones should be hidden or marked.
9. **Heartbeat gaps.** Time is not recorded in browsers that block background requests or when a learner works in another tab. That is why flags are soft. Do not use "none recorded" as proof of cheating.
10. **Cloudflare Workers Paid** only from about 7 Dec (per your plan). Free-plan CPU limits could hit the heaviest pages; watch for errors on the mission pages.
11. **Answer keys live in the database** (`assessment_item_keys`, `exercise_keys`) and therefore in every backup. Keep backups private.
